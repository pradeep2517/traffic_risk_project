# Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) Findings

## Key Visual Insights
1. **Road Type Vulnerability:** Expressways and dense city roads show higher peaks in risk variance due to speed vs. congestion trade-offs.
2. **Temporal Patterns:** Risk spikes notably during traditional rush hours (morning and evening commuter windows).
3. **Weather Impact:** Rainfall exceeding 30mm combined with waterlogging sharply drives risk scores into the High/Critical thresholds.
