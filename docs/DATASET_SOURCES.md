# Indian Road Safety & Traffic Dataset Sources

## 1. Government & Official Sources
- **Ministry of Road Transport & Highways (MoRTH):** Publishes annual reports on "Road Accidents in India", capturing state-wise, vehicle-wise, and cause-wise accident statistics.
- **National Crime Records Bureau (NCRB):** Publishes "Accidental Deaths and Suicides in India" (ADSI), containing detailed traffic collision data.
- **State Transport Departments:** Regional transport data, black-spot lists, and toll plaza traffic metrics.

## 2. Open Data & Repositories
- **Kaggle:** Indian traffic accident severity datasets, urban congestion logs, and road quality logs.
- **OpenStreetMap (OSM):** Spatial data for Indian road networks, highway classifications, and intersection layouts.
- **UCI Machine Learning Repository:** Classic traffic and vehicular sensor benchmarks.
